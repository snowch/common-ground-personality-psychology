# Personality Psychology learner-facing review

The course assumes everyday language and browser use. I applied the wording and interface rubric to the published `0921593` build and to the revision. The reading pass covered all 90 lesson pages and 60 decision feedback responses, 40 figures and research notes, glossaries, narration directions, the final case, the online companions, and the offline and download pages.

| Review area | Opening evidence | Revised learner flow |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Actions and feedback | The first decision asked for written reasoning without an entry field. **Compare reasoning** revealed feedback for the chosen option. The recall screen left its instructions unchanged after answering. | The first decision names **Check choice** and links back to the story. The transfer directions identify the writing field and explain the comparison the learner makes after checking. The recall page names **Reveal suggested answers**. Focus moves to feedback or suggested answers when they appear. |
| Definitions and evidence | The essential concept explanations shared an open recording transcript with repeated exercise text. Research panels repeated the same explanations and sample limits. Three field tasks asked learners to locate an external report without offering an example on the page. | The 30 concept pages place the complete explanation, brief definitions and cues beside the figure. Research panels keep the relevant source links and useful detail. Chapters 3, 5 and 9 provide fictional practice records with specific evidence to inspect. The records identify which purpose, population or comparability evidence remains unknown. |
| Progression and layout | Narration and Next looked equally prominent. The transcript repeated visible exercises. Suggested reasoning and self-assessment appeared in the study companion before learners attempted the case. | Each lesson stage gives one primary action. Exact exercise transcripts and supplemental figures are optional. Suggested reasoning and self-assessment have separate disclosures online and remain readable in the printable companion. The course-first header introduced in `0921593` is present in the deck, offline page and downloads. |
| Saving and access | Storage errors left the same guidance as successful saves. Offline update and continue could appear equally prominent. | A failed write reports that the changes are still in the tab. The writing field stays usable and learners can copy their draft. A successful later write clears the message. Visiting a lesson records navigation in the existing format. The offline page names the save and reopen actions, with one primary action per state. |

The browser walkthrough exercises real decisions, recall, glossary controls, recorded playback, keyboard navigation and lesson reflow. It checks all 90 lessons at 320, 390, 768, 1024 and 1440 pixels. The existing palette is checked under both system colour preferences on companion routes. A saved public profile supplied earlier written responses, checked and unchecked choices and visit history. A simulated failed write exercises retaining a tab-only response and saving it later. The offline walkthrough saves and reopens the course and removes the download while keeping progress. The revised chapter 3, 5 and 9 practice pages and study companion are represented in the downloadable PDFs. The combined deck retains its 90-page count.

The workflow runs the curriculum, recording and hosted-build checks on pull requests. The recording checks validate the original 160 audio files and their scripts. The browser and build checks establish functional behaviour. Learner understanding and teaching effectiveness remain unmeasured.
