[c02s03]  The Big Five organize broad differences in personality. They describe dimensions rather than dividing people into fixed types.   Extraversion involves sociability, assertiveness and activity. Agreeableness involves compassion, respectfulness and trust. Conscientiousness involves organization, responsibility and productive effort.   Negative emotionality concerns tendencies toward unpleasant emotions. Open-mindedness concerns curiosity, imagination and receptivity to ideas.   Names vary across instruments: negative emotionality is often called neuroticism, and open-mindedness is also called openness to experience. A score depends on the scale and reference group.   The framework summarizes patterns.
